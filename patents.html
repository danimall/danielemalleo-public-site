About this list. This page organizes the portfolio into 19 primary invention lineages using earliest priority and common subject matter, but it does not treat continuations or divisionals as mere duplicates. All 68 identified U.S. publications naming me as an inventor are listed individually because a later filing can protect a distinct claimed invention even when it shares priority with an earlier application. Representative PCT publications and selected regional records are also included; routine national translations and administrative validations are omitted. Counts and public-record statuses were reviewed on July 29, 2026 and are informational, not legal conclusions.
