How I work

Build the system. Prove it. Build the company around it.

I bring science, hardware, software, automation, and teams together into coherent platforms that work reliably in the real world.

Systems engineer at heart. Executive in scope.

Systems engineering is the discipline behind how I lead: define the problem and interfaces, surface assumptions, retire risk early, and validate with evidence. I apply the same approach to architecture, organizations, company strategy, and capital.

01 · ARCHITECT

Start from the whole system

Define the system, interfaces, and highest-risk assumptions early so individual design choices reinforce the platform rather than constrain it.

02 · BUILD

Reduce ambiguity with prototypes

Use prototypes, experiments, and direct user contact to retire the highest risks before scaling.

03 · SCALE

Grow product and company together

Build the team and operating model around the product, while creating the partnerships, IP, and financing story needed to scale.
